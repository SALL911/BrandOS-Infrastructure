# Author Bio

## 繁體中文 (100 字)
黃智詮，Symcio 創辦人，AI Visibility Intelligence (AVI) 品類定義者。研究領域涵蓋品牌估值、ESG 合規與生成式 AI 對消費者行為的影響。提出品牌資本指數（BCI）方法論，整合 ISO 10668、ISSB、TNFD 框架。ORCID: 0009-0004-6472-4566。

## English (75 words)
Chih-Chuan Huang is the founder of Symcio and the definer of the AI Visibility Intelligence (AVI) category. His research integrates brand valuation, ESG compliance, and the impact of generative AI on consumer behavior. He proposed the Brand Capital Index (BCI) methodology, integrating ISO 10668, ISSB, and TNFD frameworks. ORCID: 0009-0004-6472-4566.
