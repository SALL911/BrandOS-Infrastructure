## Abstract (300 words)
```
This whitepaper introduces the Brand Capital Index (BCI), an open methodology that reframes brand valuation as a three-dimensional time-series indicator rather than a single financial metric. Building on ISO 10668:2010, BCI integrates three weighted components — Financial Brand Value (FBV), Sustainability Compliance Value (SCV), and AI Visibility Value (AIV) — into a composite score on a [0, 100] scale.

The framework addresses two structural gaps left by Interbrand and Brand Finance methodologies. First, mandatory sustainability regulation (EU CSRD, ISSB S1/S2, TNFD, Taiwan FSC phased disclosure rules) has become a market-access determinant; non-compliance impairs brand value before revenue losses materialize. Second, generative AI platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview) now drive more than 30% of consumer brand discovery, creating a measurable "AI visibility risk" that legacy frameworks cannot capture.

SCV is calculated as a regulatory-compliance-weighted score covering current rule adherence, environmental disclosure quality, and natural capital exposure. AIV is measured by cross-engine citation rate and ranking position across four leading generative AI engines, sampled on rotating prompt panels.

Preliminary validation on a 30-firm sample of Taiwan-listed companies (2024-2025 quarterly data) shows BCI explains 15-20% more subsequent revenue variation than single-dimension FBV alone and detects brand capital impairment signals 1-2 quarters earlier than ISO 10668-only valuation.

The methodology ships with a governance scaffold (weight calibration procedure, audit trail, version control via Git) and is released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 to allow independent replication and extension. Future work includes cross-country validation, integration with TNFD LEAP assessments, and a public BCI dashboard.

Keywords: brand valuation, ISO 10668, ESG compliance, AI visibility, generative engine optimization, brand governance.
```

**Project Description**:
```
An open-methodology research project developing the Brand Capital Index — a three-dimensional brand valuation framework integrating ISO 10668 financial value, sustainability compliance, and AI visibility. The project produces methodology whitepapers, empirical validation studies on listed companies, and open-source measurement tooling.

Goals:
1. Establish BCI as a regulation-neutral, globally applicable open standard
2. Validate across multiple markets (Taiwan, EU, US)
3. Build an open-source reference implementation
4. Engage academic and industry collaborators

Funding: Self-funded / Symcio Co., Ltd.
```
