# SSRN — 直達連結

## 主要頁面
- **註冊**: https://hq.ssrn.com/Register.cfm
- **登入**: https://hq.ssrn.com/login.cfm
- **新投稿**: https://hq.ssrn.com/submission.cfm
- **我的論文**: https://hq.ssrn.com/myPapers.cfm

## 建議投稿的 Research Network
- **FEN — Financial Economics Network**: https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/en/fen/
- **MKT — Marketing Research Network**: https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/en/mkt/
- **CSN — Corporate Governance Network**: https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/en/csn/
- **ERPN — Economic Research Paper Network**: https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/en/erpn/

> 一次投稿可同時提交到多個 networks，建議勾選上述 4 個。

## 為什麼 P0
SSRN 是 Elsevier 旗下、全球最大的社會科學預印本平台。
- Finance / Marketing / Management 學者必到
- Google Scholar 自動爬 SSRN
- 投稿後 1-3 工作天通過審核，立即取得 SSRN URL
- 可累積 downloads / abstract views 作為「引用前的影響力指標」
