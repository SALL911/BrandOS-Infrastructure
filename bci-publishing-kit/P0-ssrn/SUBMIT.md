## Title
```
Brand Capital Index (BCI) Methodology Whitepaper: Integrating Financial Brand Value, Sustainability Compliance Value, and AI Visibility Value
```

## Abstract (≤2000 chars)
```
This whitepaper proposes the Brand Capital Index (BCI), an open methodology that extends brand valuation from a single financial dimension to a three-dimensional time-series indicator. The BCI formula is BCI = α·FBV + β·SCV + γ·AIV, where α + β + γ = 1 and BCI ∈ [0,100]. The three components are: (i) FBV (Financial Brand Value) — brand contribution to corporate earnings, calculated using the ISO 10668:2010 income approach; (ii) SCV (Sustainability Compliance Value) — a regulatory-risk-adjusted value integrating ISSB S1/S2, TNFD, EU CSRD, and the Taiwan FSC phased ESG disclosure rules; and (iii) AIV (AI Visibility Value) — brand citation rate and position score across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude.

BCI addresses two structural blind spots of legacy valuation frameworks (Interbrand, Brand Finance): (1) mandatory sustainability regulation has become a market-access determinant, with non-compliance creating brand value impairment before revenue losses appear in financial statements; and (2) generative AI platforms have become a primary brand-discovery channel, with over 30% of consumers using AI-driven platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview) instead of traditional search engines as of 2025.

Preliminary validation on 30 Taiwan-listed companies (2024-2025 quarterly data) shows BCI explains 15-20% more subsequent revenue variation than single-dimension FBV alone, and detects brand capital impairment signals 1-2 quarters earlier. The methodology is published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, is designed to be globally applicable and regulation-neutral, and ships with a governance scaffold including weight calibration procedures, audit trails, and version control.

Keywords: brand valuation, ISO 10668, ESG compliance, AI visibility, generative engine optimization, brand governance, ISSB, TNFD, brand capital, sustainability.
```
