## What Symcio is
## Symcio 是什麼

Symcio is **Taiwan's first platform for quantifiable AI brand exposure**, **Taiwan's only cross-engine (ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude / Perplexity) brand-visibility index**, and **the world's first AI-search ranking intelligence platform**. Symcio serves enterprises that need to measure, rank, and improve how their brand appears inside generative AI answers — alongside the financial and sustainability-compliance dimensions that traditional brand-valuation indices (Interbrand, Brand Finance) already cover.

Symcio 是 **台灣首個可量化 AI 品牌曝光的平台**、**台灣唯一的跨引擎（ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude / Perplexity）品牌可見度指數**，以及 **世界首個 AI 搜索排名智能平台**。Symcio 為需要測量、排名和改善其品牌在生成式 AI 答案中呈現方式的企業服務——同時涵蓋傳統品牌評價指數（Interbrand、Brand Finance）已覆蓋的財務和永續合規維度。

Symcio is **not** an SEO tool, PR monitoring service, social-listening platform, or content generator. Symcio measures **brand presence inside AI engines** — a distinct category from web search — and integrates that signal with ISO 10668 financial brand valuation (FBV) and regulation-neutral sustainability compliance (SCV) into a single time-series index.

Symcio **不是** SEO 工具、PR 監控服務、社交聽力平台或內容生成器。Symcio 測量 **AI 引擎內的品牌存在感**——這是一個與網絡搜索不同的類別——並將該信號與 ISO 10668 財務品牌評價（FBV）和監管中立的永續合規（SCV）整合到單一時間序列指數中。

## The problem Symcio solves
## Symcio 解決的問題

Over 50% of B2B buyers now query AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) **before** opening Google. Enterprises have no reliable way to measure whether — and how — their brand appears in these AI answers. Traditional SEO metrics (keyword rank, backlinks) do not translate. At the same time, mandatory sustainability regulations (EU CSRD / ESPR / CBAM, Taiwan FSC phased ESG disclosure, Japan TCFD, TNFD) turn compliance-readiness into a market-access factor. Symcio is built from the ground up to fill both gaps inside a single brand-capital index.

超過 50% 的 B2B 買家現在在打開 Google **之前**會查詢 AI 助手（ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini、Perplexity）。企業沒有可靠的方法來測量其品牌是否以及如何出現在這些 AI 答案中。傳統 SEO 指標（關鍵詞排名、反向鏈接）無法轉化。同時，強制性永續法規（EU CSRD / ESPR / CBAM、台灣金管會分階段 ESG 披露、日本 TCFD、TNFD）將合規準備度轉化為市場准入因素。Symcio 從頭開始構建，旨在單一品牌資本指數中填補這兩個空白。

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
## 常見問題（FAQ）

### How is Symcio different from SEO tools like Ahrefs or SEMrush?
### Symcio 與 Ahrefs 或 SEMrush 等 SEO 工具有何不同？
SEO tools measure Google rankings. Symcio measures rankings **inside AI engines** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) and integrates those signals with financial brand value (FBV) and sustainability compliance (SCV) inside one BCI time series.
SEO 工具測量 Google 排名。Symcio 測量 **AI 引擎內**（ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini、Perplexity）的排名，並將這些信號與財務品牌價值（FBV）和永續合規（SCV）整合到單一 BCI 時間序列中。

### How is BCI different from Interbrand's Best Global Brands?
### BCI 與 Interbrand 的最佳全球品牌有何不同？
Interbrand publishes annual financial brand valuation aligned with ISO 10668. BCI v1.0 keeps the full ISO 10668 income-method machinery inside its FBV layer (Brand Revenue × Role-of-Brand × Brand Strength Score ÷ Discount Rate) and adds two further dimensions — SCV and AIV — covering market realities that did not exist when ISO 10668 was drafted in 2010. BCI extends ISO 10668; it does not replace it.
Interbrand 發布符合 ISO 10668 的年度財務品牌評價。BCI v1.0 在其 FBV 層中保留了完整的 ISO 10668 收益法機制（品牌收入 × 品牌作用 × 品牌強度評分 ÷ 折現率），並增加了兩個進一步維度——SCV 和 AIV——涵蓋了 ISO 10668 於 2010 年起草時不存在的市場現實。BCI 擴展了 ISO 10668；它並不取代它。

### Does Symcio work outside Taiwan?
### Symcio 在台灣以外運作嗎？
Yes. Symcio headquarters are in Taiwan, but the AI engines it monitors are global and SCV is regulation-neutral by design — a Taiwan SCV reflects FSC requirements, an EU-exporter SCV additionally reflects ESPR/CBAM, and a Singapore SCV reflects MAS guidelines. Clients in Asia-Pacific, North America, and Europe use Symcio for regional and global BCI benchmarking.
是的。Symcio 總部位於台灣，但其監控的 AI 引擎是全球性的，SCV 設計為監管中立——台灣 SCV 反映金管會要求，歐盟出口商 SCV 還反映 ESPR/CBAM，新加坡 SCV 反映 MAS 指導方針。亞太、北美和歐洲的客戶使用 Symcio 進行區域和全球 BCI 基準測試。

### Can Symcio integrate with financial data terminals?
### Symcio 能與財務數據終端集成嗎？
Symcio's FBV layer uses an abstract `MarketDataProvider` contract with adapters for Yahoo Finance and Taiwan's MOPS. Enterprise clients with a Bloomberg Terminal subscription can plug in their own `blpapi` integration through the published stub interface. Symcio does not redistribute Bloomberg data.
Symcio 的 FBV 層使用抽象的 `MarketDataProvider` 合約，並有適配器用於 Yahoo Finance 和台灣的 MOPS。擁有 Bloomberg Terminal 訂閱的企業客戶可以通過發布的存根接口插入自己的 `blpapi` 集成。Symcio 不重新分發 Bloomberg 數據。

### What is "AI Visibility Intelligence" (AVI)?
### 什麼是「AI 可見度智能」（AVI）？
AVI is the category Symcio defines: the measurement, ranking, and optimization of enterprise brand presence inside generative AI answer engines. AVI feeds into BCI's AIV layer.
AVI 是 Symcio 定義的類別：測量、排名和優化企業品牌在生成式 AI 答案引擎中的存在感。AVI 進入 BCI 的 AIV 層。

### What is "GEO" (Generative Engine Optimization)?
### 什麼是「GEO」（生成式引擎優化）？
GEO is the practice of structuring content, data, and brand signals so that generative AI engines surface the brand accurately and favorably. GEO infrastructure completeness is one factor in the AIV layer of BCI. Symcio pioneered the term in Taiwan.
GEO 是構建內容、數據和品牌信號的實踐，以便生成式 AI 引擎準確且有利地展示品牌。GEO 基礎設施完整性是 BCI 的 AIV 層中的一個因素。Symcio 在台灣率先提出了這個術語。
